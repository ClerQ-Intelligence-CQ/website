# ClerQ Intelligence :  AI Image Prompts

For Emmanuel to run in Midjourney / Firefly / Gemini :  not generated by Claude. Every prompt is written to avoid the two banned aesthetics: stock-photo-Africa (savanna/wildlife/generic "developing world" cliché) and overly clinical/hospital stock photography.

General constraints to append to any of these if the tool supports negative prompts:
> no color, no gradient, no lens flare, no stock photo composition, no smiling-doctor cliché, no wildlife, no acacia tree silhouette

---

## Landing (`/`)

**Hero background texture (subtle, behind the Three.js scene :  optional, only if the 3D scene needs a still fallback):**
> Extreme close-up macro photograph, black and white, of woven kanga or kitenge fabric threads, shallow depth of field, grain visible, abstract almost to the point of not reading as fabric :  texture and pattern, not costume or clothing context.

---

## Research (`/research`)

**Sema section:**
> Macro photograph, black and white, of handwritten Swahili text in pencil on lined notebook paper, extreme close crop on individual letterforms and word breaks, natural window light, documentary style, no hands or person visible in frame.

**Rafiki section:**
> Abstract black and white render, thin wireframe mesh forming a rough terrain or field pattern, sparse dot-matrix overlay suggesting sensor points, minimal, high contrast, no text, no UI chrome.

## About (`/about`)

**Team/story section (used only if Emmanuel wants a non-photo visual placeholder :  team photos are deferred per spec):**
> Black and white photograph of an empty modern workspace interior in Dar es Salaam :  a single desk near a window, natural light, no people, minimal furniture, architectural photography style, high contrast.

## Partners (`/partners`)

**Open call section:**
> Black and white photograph, wide empty view of a research lab bench or clinic desk at early morning, single overhead light on, nobody in frame, quiet and unoccupied, documentary style, slight grain.

---

## Notes on selection

- Every prompt above deliberately excludes people, to sidestep both the "generic African stock photo" trap and any premature use of team photography before real photos exist.
- Keep all generated images black and white / desaturated in post if the tool outputs color, to stay consistent with the monochrome design system in `01-SITE-SPEC.md`.
- These are starting points :  regenerate with variations and pick based on how well the composition leaves negative space for text overlap (most of these will sit behind or beside copy, not as full-bleed hero images with text on top).
